FOTRIVA Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026 · version 1.1.13 · Политика конфиденциальности (RU)

FOTRIVA is an app for managing photo shoots. It works without an account and without developer servers: all data stays on your device.

Data processed

Clients, projects, payment stages, the financial journal, tax profiles and settings. You create this data; it is stored in the app's local database on the device. The developer has no access to it.

Permissions

Calendar — to show shoots in the device calendar and import events. Contacts — to add a client from your address book. Notifications — to remind you about shoots, editing and self-employed tax receipts. Each permission is requested only when you start the related feature; denying it does not break other features. Before the system dialog, the app shows an in-app explanation of what data will be read and why.

Calendar sync

If you connect Yandex Calendar or Google Calendar, events are sent to your own account over HTTPS and only after your action. The app does not get access to other account data. Yandex app passwords and Google tokens are stored in Android Keystore and are not included in backups.

Backups

.fotrivabak archives are written to a folder you choose via the system folder picker (for example Google Drive or Yandex Drive). The app does not access your cloud account. An archive can be protected with a password; without it the archive cannot be opened.

Device code and the Pro licence

The "device code" on the "Settings → FOTRIVA Pro" screen is derived from the Android installation identifier (`ANDROID_ID`) and the package name. On Android 8 and newer `ANDROID_ID` is unique for the pair "app + user": it does not change when the app is reinstalled with the same signing key, but it differs across devices and across apps.

This is not a hardware fingerprint: the app does not read serial numbers, processor identifiers or any other device characteristics. The raw `ANDROID_ID` is not stored — only its hash combined with the package name. The code itself does not leave the device until you send it to the seller, who puts it into the issued key so that the key works on that device.

When a key is activated, and when the "FOTRIVA Pro" screen is opened, the app may request the file with the list of revoked keys from the FOTRIVA website. It is a plain download of one text file; your clients, projects and other data are not part of it and are not sent anywhere. If there is no network, the list bundled with the app is used.

Analytics, ads and data sharing

The app contains no ads, analytics SDKs or trackers. Data is not sold and is not transferred to third parties, except the calendar sync, the backup file described above and the download of the revoked-key list.

Purchase request on the website

If you send a purchase request through the FOTRIVA website, your name and e-mail are sent over HTTPS to the form delivery service Formspree, which delivers the message to the developer. These details are used only to reply to the request and to issue the invoice. The website contains no analytics or advertising trackers.

Deletion

Data can be deleted inside the app (clients, projects) or by uninstalling it: the local database is removed with the app. Backup archives remain in your folder until you delete them yourself.

Contact

Data protection questions: fotriva@gmail.com or fotriva@yandex.ru. The developer contact is also listed on the FOTRIVA page in Google Play.
